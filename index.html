MY TRAVEL BLOG

Welcome to my travel blog! I'll be sharing my experience at the Amboseli National Park. This park is well known for its stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro and its large elephant herds. During my visit, I had the opportunity to witness the breathtaking sunset over the savannah, which was truly a sight to behold. The park is also home to a variety of wildlife, including lions, cheetahs, and giraffes. I highly recommend visiting Amboseli National Park for anyone looking to experience the beauty of nature and wildlife in Africa. It's definently the go to place for you and your entire family during the winter holidays in Kenya.
